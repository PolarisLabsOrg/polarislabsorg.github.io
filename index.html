FAQ

Questions & answers

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What makes Horizon Africa different from other counselling services?
▾
We're alumni-directed with direct experience at top universities. We provide comprehensive, end-to-end support — not just essay reviews. Our track record speaks: $1.9M+ in scholarships secured and students placed at Harvard, Yale, Princeton, MIT, and Stanford.
Do you really help with SAT/ACT preparation?
▾
Yes! We provide full preparation and support for SAT/ACT examinations as part of our commitment to removing financial barriers to world-class education. This includes personalised study plans, practice materials, and ongoing coaching.
What's included in the program?
▾
Everything: SAT/ACT prep and funding, unlimited essay revisions, financial aid strategy (CSS Profile & ISFAA), interview coaching with alumni, application support, waitlist letter assistance, and full visa guidance. It's a complete, end-to-end package from first assessment to landing on campus.
When should I start the program?
▾
The earlier the better! Ideally start 8–12 months before application deadlines. This allows time for thorough SAT preparation, essay development, and financial aid optimisation. However, we can also work with tighter timelines — book a free call and we'll assess your situation.
How flexible is the payment plan?
▾
We offer flexible, dynamic payment plans to make our program accessible for families committed to investing in education. The goal is to remove barriers, not create them. Discuss your situation on a free consultation call and we'll find a structure that works.
Do you work with students outside Kenya?
▾
Absolutely. While we're based in Nairobi, we work with students across the African continent. All mentoring and coaching sessions can be conducted virtually, and we have experience navigating applications from multiple African countries.
