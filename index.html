§1The Process

Five stages, one dossier

Each engagement moves through a fixed sequence — the same discipline an admissions committee expects of a finished application.

01

Assessment & Strategy

A candid profile review, shortlist of institutions matched to fit and funding likelihood, and the opening of standardized-test preparation.

02

Essay Development

Structured brainstorming and unlimited revision with alumni mentors until the personal statement carries a distinct, defensible voice.

03

Applications & Aid

Submission of every component alongside the CSS Profile and ISFAA, positioned to maximise need-based support.

04

Interviews

Recorded mock sessions with alumni interviewers and structured feedback ahead of every scheduled conversation.

05

Visa & Departure

F-1 documentation, embassy interview preparation, and a departure briefing for the first weeks on campus.

§5Questions on Record

Frequently asked

What most families ask before their first consultation.

What distinguishes Horizon Africa from other counselling services?+
We are alumni-directed, with direct experience inside the institutions you're applying to, and we provide comprehensive support rather than essay review alone. Our record: $1.9M+ in aid secured and placements across Harvard, Yale, Princeton, MIT, and Stanford.
Do you genuinely support SAT/ACT preparation?+
Yes. We provide full preparation and funding for standardized testing as part of removing financial barriers to entry — personalised study plans, materials, and ongoing coaching included.
What exactly is included in the program?+
Testing prep and funding, unlimited essay revisions, financial aid strategy (CSS Profile & ISFAA), alumni interview coaching, full application support, waitlist strategy, and visa guidance — start to finish, one file.
When should a student open their file with us?+
Ideally 8–12 months ahead of deadlines, to allow proper time for testing, essays, and aid strategy. Tighter timelines can still be worked — bring your situation to a consultation and we'll assess it directly.
How flexible are your payment terms?+
We structure flexible, dynamic payment plans for families committed to the process. The intent is to remove barriers, not create new ones — this is discussed directly on your consultation call.
Do you work with students outside Kenya?+
Yes — we are based in Nairobi but work with students across the African continent. All mentoring is conducted virtually, with experience navigating applications from multiple countries.
